FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is forex trading, and how do I get started?
Forex trading means buying one currency while selling another, aiming to profit from changes in exchange rates. To get started, open an account with a regulated broker like Pepperstone, fund it, and begin trading. We recommend practising on a demo account first.
What is a 'spread' and why does it matter?
The spread is the gap between the buy and sell price of an instrument — it's one of your main trading costs. Tighter spreads mean lower costs per trade. Pepperstone's Razor account offers spreads from 0.0 pips on major pairs.
How much do I need to start trading?
Pepperstone has no minimum deposit requirement, so you can start with whatever amount suits your situation. If you're new, begin small and take advantage of the free demo account to build confidence.
What is a demo account and should I use one?
A demo account lets you trade in live market conditions using virtual funds — zero financial risk. It's the ideal way to learn the platform and test strategies before trading with real money.
Which trading platform should I choose?
It depends on your experience and needs. TradingView is great for charting and community insights; cTrader appeals to algo traders; MT4/MT5 are versatile all-rounders. Pepperstone supports all four, so you can switch any time.
What are the risks of forex trading?
Forex trading uses leverage, which can amplify both gains and losses. Market volatility and liquidity gaps are real risks. Always trade with a stop-loss, size your positions carefully, and never risk money you can't afford to lose.
